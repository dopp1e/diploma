\chapter{INTRODUCTION (Jakub Kinder)}

% Introducing idea of PC building and its versitality
In recent years there has been a significant surge in interest in assembling custom PCs. 
While initially popularized by the video game development industry, whose products demand an increasingly sophisticated and powerful hardware, more people began to notice vast benefits of a custom-built PC.
With proper knowledge and skills, one can tailor a computer to precisely fit their needs regarding performance or aesthetics and at the same time, reduce overall cost of the machine, by prioritizing only certain components.
For instance, a professional graphic designer may require a device with superior Graphic Processing Unit (GPU) for tasks such as rendering images and doesn't have much use for full capabilities of newest Central Processing Unit (CPU).
However, PC building, while simple, requires rather comprehensive knowledge, not precisely of inner workings of a computer, but rather the parts themselves.
It is incredibly crucial to know various types of ports, their versions and how to connect them properly so as not to damage them or components that make up a PC.
Unfortunately, with aforementioned growth of popularity of the idea, the knowledge, skills or even means on how to gain them, were not promoted.
That lead many people to abandon the idea of building their own PC, because not only components chosen must be compatible with each other, but also they tend to be expensive and relatively easy to damage, due to lack of information.

% Introduction to tools and simulators that were developed
In response to lack of sources on the topic, countless tools have been developed with the goal of helping people design or build their own custom PC.
Most of them, however, focus on the subject of component selection, especially compatibility regarding standards of ports, without taking into account their physical size.
Nonetheless, they have still proven useful for beginners, through reducing knowledge required in order to at least choose adequate hardware configuration.
Apart from them, there is a niche category of tools that promotes a more in depth approach to spreading the knowledge on PC building.
Such tools took on a form of simulation games, from which the most well known is PC Building Simulator \cite{pcbuilder}, the first installment of entire series of such games.
They differ from previously mentioned tools, by providing not only knowledge of proper component selection, but also how assembly itself looks like in 3D environment.
However, these tools are still a simplification of reality, they may offer glimpses into the process of building a PC or reduce knowledge needed to select components, but they still cannot provide necessary skills.

% Introduction to vr technology and cave
There is one possible and very promising solution to this particular dilemma, though currently unexplored in this context, it is Virtual Reality (VR).
This technology has made strides in its development over the years, especially when it comes to games of various genres, which includes highly interactive simulations.
Moreover, VR is extremely immersive and most actions performed within its environment are intuitive or demand only minimal adjustments from users.
These characteristics alone make it more than a prime choice of the environment for development of new tools for both experienced and novice PC builders.
However, there is an environment that is capable of far superior immersion and interactivity than classic VR, it is called CAVE Automatic Virtual Environment (CAVE).
This technology, surpasses regular VR set in almost every aspect, instead of displaying digital world from user's eye level, user's entire body is placed inside the virtual space.
These circumstances bolster immersion, by making user aware of all their movements, thus allowing for acquirement of hands-on experience with tasks that are included in given simulation.

% Introduction to the goal of the project
The goal of this project is to take advantage of aforementioned unexplored environment as well as lack of comprehensive tools to learn PC building, and develop an extensive PC building simulator in VR environment, specifically CAVE.
Such a solution will be the first of its kind, and not only will it show in depth how to assemble a PC or how the whole process looks like, but also it will promote more practical skills to a certain degree.
Besides allowing users to learn, the developed application will give them the opportunity to test various configurations of components, an aspect which both novice and experienced users will benefit from.
In addition, to aforementioned features, another aim of the solution will involve training users to complete assembly of a PC in timely manner.
Apart from developing an application mentioned above, another main goal is to perform a short study on small group of testers with varying levels of experience regarding building PCs.
It will consist of a survey followed by a session in developed simulator and a comparison session utilizing existing solution in classic 3D environment.
