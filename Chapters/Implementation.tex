\subsubsection{3D models (Hubert Leszczyński)}
Most of the classes \ref{sec:classes} required creating appropriate 3D models.
The computer component models were created based on their real counterparts.
The first step of creating each model of a computer component was measuring it's real dimensions in three axis.
Then, the prototypes of the models were designed in blender using simple geometric solids.
The prototypes were then subjected to the necessary modifications, and finally the appropriate materials were applied to them.
Figure \ref{fig:ModelExample} shows an example model of a computer component, in this case a disk.
