\chapter*{Abstract}

Today's computer component market is developing very dynamically, which leads to an increasing number of their types, models or configurations.
Assembling a personal computer might seem like a complicated task, considering hardware compatibility and differences in the individual components construction.
One of the solutions to these problems may be an application that introduces its users to the subject of computer assembly.

The objective of this diploma thesis was to design and implement a virtual trainer operating in the virtual reality caves located at the Immersive 3D Visualization Lab at Gdańsk University of Technology.
As a result of our work, a computer building simulator was created, offering its users the virtual assembly of multiple hardware configurations, considering the compatibility of the components, and guiding them through the entire process in a realistic way.
The application provides its users an immersion in the environment resembling a real-world room.
The trainer was implemented with the necessary mechanics for full control over computer components, allowing the trainee to move and connect them.
After creating the simulator, it was thoroughly tested in the virtual reality caves, and the research was conducted to assess its intuitiveness and educational value.
